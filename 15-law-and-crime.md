## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **法律话题必备动词搭配**：commit a crime（犯罪）、face charges（面临指控）、plead guilty/not guilty（认罪/不认罪）、serve a sentence（服刑）、press charges（提起诉讼）——这5组动宾搭配是写作和口语的骨架，熟记后换词填空即可。

2. **替换高频但薄弱的词**：写作中不要只用 punish，可替换为 impose a penalty / hand down a sentence / subject to retribution；不要只用 lawyer，出庭律师用 attorney（美式）或 barrister（英式）更精准。

3. **高频雅思写作句型**：
   - "The presumption of innocence is a cornerstone of any fair **judiciary** system."
   - "Stricter penalties serve as a **deterrence** against white-collar **fraud** and **embezzlement**."
   - "**Rehabilitation** programmes have been shown to reduce **recidivism** rates among juvenile offenders."

## 📱 小红书发布文案

**标题：** 雅思8.0词汇图鉴 15｜法律与犯罪

**正文：**

写到 crime、law、social issues 这类话题，是不是只会写 criminal、police、punishment 三个词转来转去？

fraud 欺诈、forensic 法证的、recidivism 累犯、exonerate 免罪……这些词你看到认识，自己写的时候脑子里一片空白——这5张卡片专门解决这个问题。

按"法庭→犯罪→调查→权利→量刑"5条逻辑线来分类，每张卡15-16个词，意思相近的聚在一起，用的时候直接调出来。

**卡片1｜法庭程序**
→ judiciary 司法机构 / verdict 裁决 / acquittal 无罪开释 / perjury 伪证罪

**卡片2｜犯罪类型**
→ fraud 欺诈 / homicide 杀人罪 / conspiracy 共谋 / culpable 有罪的

**卡片3｜执法调查**
→ forensic 法证的 / surveillance 监控 / alibi 不在场证明 / incriminate 使入罪

**卡片4｜法律权利**
→ presumption 推定 / exonerate 免罪 / affidavit 宣誓书 / mitigate 减轻

**卡片5｜量刑改造**
→ incarceration 监禁 / parole 假释 / recidivism 累犯 / clemency 宽大处理

**写作高频搭配，直接套用：**

① **commit a premeditated crime** — The offender was found guilty of committing a premeditated homicide.

② **be presumed innocent** — Under the law, all defendants are presumed innocent until proven guilty beyond reasonable doubt.

③ **serve as a deterrence** — Heavier penalties for fraud are believed to serve as a deterrence for potential white-collar criminals.

④ **reduce recidivism** — Effective rehabilitation programmes in prison are key to reducing recidivism among young offenders.

收藏这组，下次写到 crime / justice / social issues 话题，开口就能用 forensic evidence、criminal prosecution、parole conditions，不用再靠 bad people go to jail 撑场面了。
