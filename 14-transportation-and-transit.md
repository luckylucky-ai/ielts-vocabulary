## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 区分易混搭配**
- **road vs. motorway**：road 泛指道路；motorway 特指多车道高速公路，相当于美式英语 freeway/highway。写作时描述「城市道路扩建」用 road infrastructure；描述「城际快速路」用 motorway。
- **delay vs. suspension**：delay 是延误（还会运行，只是晚点）；suspension 是停运（服务暂停）。雅思写作描述交通问题时区分两者更精准。

**2. 高频动词搭配记忆**
- ease / relieve / alleviate + **congestion**（缓解拥堵）
- reduce / cut / lower + **emissions**（减少排放）
- improve + **connectivity / punctuality / frequency**（提升连通性/准时率/班次密度）
- invest in + **infrastructure / electrification**（投资基础设施/电气化）

**3. 口语 Part 2 万能句型**
描述交通问题时可用：
*"The sheer volume of private vehicles has led to chronic congestion and gridlock, particularly at major junctions during peak hours."*
描述解决方案时可用：
*"Governments should incentivise the use of zero-emission vehicles through subsidies and invest in the electrification of public transport to decarbonise the sector."*
