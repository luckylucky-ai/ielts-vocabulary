## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：讨论物种保护议题时，用 "habitat fragmentation has pushed the population below a viable threshold, triggering a genetic bottleneck" 比 "the forest was cut down and animals became fewer" 更学术；讨论顶级捕食者的作用时，"the removal of an apex predator can trigger a trophic cascade throughout the entire ecosystem" 是 Task 2 环保类的高分句型。
2. **口语加分技巧**：描述动物行为时，别再只说 clever/smart，试试 "elephants show remarkable empathy and even a degree of self-awareness" 或 "wolves rely on cooperative hunting rather than solitary pursuit"，这类词能明显提升 Lexical Resource 得分。
3. **区分近义词**：`extirpate`（局部灭绝，某地区消失但物种仍存在于别处）vs `functional extinction`（种群数量太少已无法维持生态功能）；`sedate` vs `tranquilize` 都指"使镇静"，但 tranquilize 常特指用麻醉枪等方式使动物失去行动能力。阅读题常在这类近义词的细微差别上设陷阱。
