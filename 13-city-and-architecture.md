## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 高频搭配别死背，成对记忆更高效**
城市话题的高分搭配往往成对出现：
- *urban sprawl* 对应 *compact city* 紧凑城市
- *residential zone* 对应 *commercial precinct* 商业区域
- *ease congestion* 对应 *improve mobility* 提升流动性
- *impose zoning regulations* — 实施分区规定（写作常考）

**2. 雅思写作万能句型（城市话题）**
- "The rapid **urbanisation** of major cities has placed immense pressure on **infrastructure**, exacerbating traffic **congestion** and reducing urban **greenspace**."
- "**Gentrification** of inner-city areas has transformed previously deprived **neighbourhoods** but simultaneously led to the **displacement** of long-term residents."
- "Investing in **renewable** energy and **photovoltaic** technology can significantly reduce carbon **emissions** while enhancing a city's **resilience** to climate change."

**3. 口语 Part 3 答题升级词**
普通答法："There's too much traffic." → 升级版："Urban **congestion** stems largely from overdependence on private vehicles and inadequate **transit** infrastructure, undermining city **mobility**."
普通答法："Old buildings are important." → 升级版："Preserving architectural **heritage** maintains a city's cultural identity, while **retrofit** projects ensure these structures meet contemporary **structural** and energy standards."
