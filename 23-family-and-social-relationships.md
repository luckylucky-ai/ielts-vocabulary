## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 高频写作搭配，直接套用**

- **foster strong family bonds** 培养强大的家庭纽带
  → *Cultural traditions help foster strong family bonds, reinforcing a shared sense of identity across generations.*

- **bridge the generation gap** 弥合代沟
  → *Open communication between parents and their offspring is essential to bridge the generation gap in modern societies.*

- **shoulder domestic obligations** 承担家庭义务
  → *In many households, women continue to shoulder a disproportionate share of domestic obligations despite advances in gender equality.*

**2. 写作替换升级**

| 低分表达 | 高分替换 |
|---------|---------|
| big family | extended family |
| love each other | cherish a deep sense of mutual affection |
| parents teach children | parental upbringing shapes offspring's values |
| good relationship | strong kinship bonds / enduring solidarity |
| parents and children don't understand each other | a widening generation gap |

**3. 口语 Part 1/2 快速调用**

Speaking Part 1 回答 "Describe your family":
> *"I come from a nuclear family, but we maintain close kinship ties with our extended family. My parents have always been incredibly supportive, and I'd say the camaraderie among my siblings is one of the things I cherish most."*
