## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 高频写作搭配**
- **a celestial body** 天体（固定表达，比"star/planet"更学术）
- **the gravitational pull / force of** ... 的引力（高频名词短语）
- **orbit the Earth / sun** 绕轨道运行（orbit 作动词，很实用）
- **launch a satellite / spacecraft into orbit** 将卫星/航天器送入轨道

**2. 雅思写作范句**
- *The discovery of exoplanets within the **habitable zone** of distant stars has reignited debate about the existence of extraterrestrial life.*
  （适合"科技与未来"类话题，展示词汇广度）
- *Advances in **spectroscopy** have enabled astronomers to **detect** the chemical composition of stellar atmospheres from billions of light-years away.*
  （高分特征：复杂名词短语 + 不定式状语）

**3. 口语 Part 3 快速上手**
- 题目：*Do you think space exploration is worth the enormous cost?*
- 答题框架：让步（the financial cost is astronomical）→ 转折（long-term benefits outweigh short-term expense）→ 举例（satellite technology underpins GPS, weather forecasting, and global communications）
- 加分词汇：**trajectory of scientific progress / interplanetary missions / rover data**
