## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：描述极端天气影响时，用 "unprecedented rainfall has left low-lying infrastructure highly vulnerable to flooding" 比 "it rained a lot and caused damage" 更学术；描述应对措施用 "retrofitting older buildings has significantly improved their resilience to extreme weather"。
2. **口语加分技巧**：Part 1/2 谈天气话题时，别再只说 nice/bad weather，用 "it's been quite unsettled lately — balmy one day, then bleak and rainy the next" 或 "the afternoons get rather stifling and oppressive in summer"，能明显提升 Lexical Resource 得分。
3. **Task 1 图表常客**：描述气候带对比图时，arid / tropical / continental / alpine / tundra 一整套气候区词几乎是标配；描述天气预报或灾害应对流程图时，early warning / contingency / evacuate / relief 是高频动作链条词。

天气话题看似简单，但雅思口语 Part 1 几乎每场都会问，很多人张口还是只有 sunny、rainy、cold 这几个词，说三句就卡壳。stifling（闷热难耐的）、unsettled（多变的）、torrential（倾盆的）、resilience（韧性）……这些词平时看新闻天气预报总能刷到脸熟，真要自己开口说，却总想不起来怎么用。

这期用「场景连续画面法」整理了5张卡：把云层变化、气压系统、气候带、灾害应对、天气习语分别放进一幅连续画面里记，词跟着画面走，比单独背词表更容易形成记忆线索。

卡片1｜云层与降水机制
从 cumulus 积云到 cumulonimbus 积雨云，把云是怎么变成雨的这条线讲清楚。

卡片2｜气压系统与风
anticyclone、jet stream、trade wind——天气预报里常听到但很少有人真正会用的风相关词。

卡片3｜气候类型与区域
arid、tropical、tundra、biome，写"某地区气候如何影响生活"类话题的高阶词。

卡片4｜极端天气应对与韧性
resilience、infrastructure、retrofit、displacement，写自然灾害类话题的加分词。

卡片5｜天气习语与描述性表达
balmy、bleak、clammy、hazy，口语描述天气不再只会说 nice 和 bad。

写作与口语高频搭配精选：

① unprecedented rainfall
"Unprecedented rainfall has left low-lying infrastructure highly vulnerable to flooding."

② retrofit for resilience
"Retrofitting older buildings has significantly improved their resilience to extreme weather events."

③ unsettled and changeable
"The weather here has been rather unsettled lately — balmy one day, then bleak and rainy the next."

④ stifling and oppressive
"The afternoons get rather stifling and oppressive in midsummer, even with the windows open."

收藏这组，下次口语聊到天气，或写作提到 climate change / natural disasters，就不用只会说 nice weather 和 it rained a lot 了。
