## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 高频动词搭配，写作必备**
- **emit / release** greenhouse gases（排放温室气体）
- **contaminate** groundwater / soil / air（污染地下水/土壤/空气）
- **enforce** environmental legislation（执行环保法规）
- **mitigate** the effects of climate change（缓解气候变化影响）
- **offset** carbon emissions by planting trees（通过植树抵消碳排放）

**2. 区分易混词**
- **pollution** = 整体状态（pollution levels are rising）
- **pollutant** = 具体污染物（chemical pollutants in rivers）
- **contaminate**（动词）→ **contamination**（名词）→ **contaminated**（形容词）
- **deforestation**（结果）vs **clearance**（动作过程）

**3. 口语话题答题句型**
- "One major cause of environmental degradation is the combustion of fossil fuels, which releases toxic pollutants and greenhouse gases into the atmosphere."
- "Governments need to ratify international treaties and enforce strict compliance to achieve meaningful mitigation of climate change."
- "Individuals can reduce their carbon footprint by adopting a zero-waste lifestyle and supporting advocacy for greener policy."
