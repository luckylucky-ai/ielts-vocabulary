## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **高频搭配 — 课程类**：雅思写作中 curriculum 和 syllabus 不可互换——curriculum 指整体课程体系，syllabus 指单门课的大纲。高分句型：*"a well-rounded curriculum that fosters both academic and extracurricular development"*（兼顾学业与课外成长的全面课程体系）。

2. **写作升级**：讨论教育话题时，避免重复用 learn/study，可替换为：*acquire knowledge, foster understanding, cultivate skills, enhance proficiency, immerse oneself in*。例句：*University education should foster analytical thinking rather than mere knowledge retention.*

3. **口语高分表达**：
   - "There's a growing disparity between schools in urban and rural areas." → 雅思 Speaking Part 3 常见话题一句话拿分
   - "Teachers should provide constructive feedback to nurture students' autonomy." → Part 3 讨论教学方式时直接用
   - "A conducive learning environment stimulates students' inquisitive mindset." → Task 2 写教育类作文时替换 "good learning environment"
