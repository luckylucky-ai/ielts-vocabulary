## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：讨论污染治理时，"retrofitting factories with scrubbers and carbon capture technology is a viable solution to meeting stricter emission standards" 比 "factories should use new machines to reduce pollution" 更学术；讨论生态系统风险时，"once an ecosystem crosses a critical threshold, the resulting collapse is often abrupt and irreversible" 是 Task 2 环境类话题升级论证深度的高分句型。
2. **口语加分技巧**：谈到"谁最先受污染影响"这类话题时，别再只说 "poor people are affected more"，试试 "low-income communities are often disproportionately exposed to toxic dumping, which raises serious questions of environmental justice"；谈到"如何减少浪费"时，可以说 "a circular economy replaces the take-make-dispose model with closed-loop systems of material recovery"，这类表达能明显提升 Lexical Resource 得分。
3. **区分近义词**：`remediation`（修复治理，强调对已污染环境的技术性清理）vs `restoration`（恢复，强调让生态系统回到接近原有的健康状态，范围更广）；`resilience`（韧性，系统承受冲击后仍能恢复的能力）vs `buffer capacity`（缓冲能力，特指系统在达到临界点前能吸收扰动的余量），两者常在雅思阅读的生态学篇章中被放在一起对比考查。
