## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **绘画类高频搭配**：abstract composition（抽象构图）、bold brushstroke（大胆笔触）、aesthetic appeal（美学吸引力）。写作中避免只写"beautiful"，试试 *The artist's use of bold brushstrokes and evocative chiaroscuro transforms a simple canvas into a transcendent masterpiece.*

2. **音乐与文化类高频搭配**：rise to a crescendo（达到高潮）、virtuoso performance（大师级演奏）、preservation of cultural heritage（文化遗产保护）、government patronage（政府资助）。雅思 Task 2 常考话题句型：*Government patronage plays a crucial role in ensuring the preservation of intangible cultural heritage that might otherwise be lost to future generations.*

3. **口语 Part 2 艺术话题救急词**：如果描述一件艺术品或演出，用 evocative / theatrical / avant-garde 替换 interesting；用 choreography / stagecraft 替换 performance；用 retrospective 描述一场回顾展览，立刻提升词汇印象分。
