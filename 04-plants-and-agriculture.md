## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 高频搭配记忆法**
"Plants & Agriculture" 话题中有几组搭配雅思写作高频出现，建议整组记忆：
- **crop rotation** → reduces soil degradation（轮作有助于减少土壤退化）
- **food security** → threatened by climate change / monoculture（粮食安全受气候变化/单一种植威胁）
- **sustainable farming practices** → preserve biodiversity（可持续农耕保护生物多样性）

**2. 写作句型模板**
- 议论文高分句：*"The widespread use of agrochemicals, while boosting short-term yield enhancement, poses significant risks to soil biodiversity and long-term agricultural resilience."*
- 对比句型：*"Unlike conventional intensive farming, organic farming prioritises soil health over immediate productivity."*

**3. 口语话题延伸**
Part 2 描述类题（如 "Describe a place in the countryside"）可自然融入 arable land / orchard / crop fields 等词。Part 3 讨论类题常涉及 food security 与 GMO (genetically modified organisms) 的利弊，建议提前准备正反论点各两个。
