## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：讨论贫富差距用 "the widening wealth disparity between the affluent and the underprivileged" 比 "rich people and poor people" 更学术；讨论社会流动性用 "limited social mobility perpetuates a cycle of poverty across generations"。
2. **口语加分技巧**：Part 3 谈社会公平问题时，用 "systemic discrimination against marginalized groups"、"grassroots campaigns pushing for legislative reform" 代替 "some people are treated unfairly"，能明显提升 Lexical Resource 分数。
3. **同义替换练习**：把 "old people live alone and society doesn't have enough money for them" 改写为 "an aging population coupled with a rising dependency ratio has placed unprecedented strain on pension systems and intergenerational caregiving" —— 一句话串起 aging population / dependency ratio / pension / intergenerational / caregiver 五个高阶词。

## 📱 小红书发布文案

**系列标题：** 雅思8.0词汇图鉴 30｜社会问题

**吸引力标题：** 雅思作文还在写 rich people 和 poor people？试试这些7分替换词

**正文：**

社会问题是雅思写作和口语里几乎年年会碰到的大类话题——贫富差距、老龄化、性别平等、住房危机、社会运动，每个子话题都有一套专属词汇，光靠 poor、old、unfair 这几个词根本撑不起论述。wealth disparity（财富差距）、dependency ratio（抚养比）、glass ceiling（玻璃天花板）、gentrification（士绅化）……这些词看中文能懂，写作文时却总想不起对应的英文。

这期用「场景聚类法」整理了5张卡：把同一场景里的词绑在一起记，词义、音标、画面一起进脑子，比孤立背词表更容易留住。

卡片1｜贫困与阶层不平等
从 poverty 贫困到 wealth disparity 财富差距，把"贫富差距怎么形成、怎么破"这条线的词一次说清楚。

卡片2｜老龄化与代际问题
aging population、dependency ratio、caregiver、intergenerational —— 描述"老龄化社会谁来养老"的核心词都在这里。

卡片3｜性别与种族平等
从 glass ceiling 玻璃天花板到 systemic bias 系统性偏见，覆盖职场与社会公平的高频考点。

卡片4｜无家可归与住房危机
homelessness、eviction、gentrification、affordable housing，写住房类议论文直接能用。

卡片5｜社会运动与公民行动
grassroots campaign、civil disobedience、lobby、collective action，讨论社会变革如何发生的核心词一网打尽。

高频写作搭配：

① the widening wealth disparity
"The widening wealth disparity between the affluent and the underprivileged has become one of the most pressing issues of our time."

② limited social mobility
"Limited social mobility means that children born into poverty often remain trapped in the same cycle as their parents."

③ systemic discrimination
"Systemic discrimination against marginalized groups continues to limit their access to education and employment."

④ grassroots campaigns
"Grassroots campaigns have proven effective in pushing governments toward meaningful legislative reform."

收藏这组，下次写到 poverty / inequality / social issues 相关话题，就不用只会写 rich and poor 了。
