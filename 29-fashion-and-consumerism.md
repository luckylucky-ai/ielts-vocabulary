## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：讨论时尚消费话题时，"the rise of fast fashion has led to unprecedented levels of overproduction and waste" 比 "people buy too many clothes" 更学术；描述消费心理用 "impulse buying driven by peer pressure and status symbols" 能同时覆盖心理学和社会学角度。
2. **口语加分技巧**：Part 3 谈广告和消费者行为时，用 "manipulate consumer behavior through subliminal advertising"、"planned obsolescence forces people to overspend" 代替 "ads trick people"，能明显提升 Lexical Resource 分数。
3. **同义替换练习**：把 "fast fashion is bad for the environment and people buy too much" 改写为 "the labor-intensive, mass-produced nature of fast fashion drives overconsumption while contaminating the environment through non-biodegradable dyes" —— 一句话串起 labor-intensive / mass-produced / overconsumption / contaminate / biodegradable 五个高阶词。

## 📱 小红书发布文案

**系列标题：** 雅思8.0词汇图鉴 29｜时尚与消费

**吸引力标题：** 雅思写作时尚消费类高分词：这18个直接能用

**正文：**

时尚与消费是雅思写作和口语里"看似简单、实则很难拿高分"的话题——很多人只会写 clothes、shopping、buy 这几个词，讨论到产业链和消费心理就没词了。sweatshop（血汗工厂）、impulse buying（冲动购物）、planned obsolescence（计划性淘汰）、commodify（商品化）……这些词能理解中文意思，但写作文时很难自然调出来。

这期用「场景聚类法」整理了5张卡：把同一场景里的词绑在一起记，词义、音标、画面一起进脑子，比孤立背词表更容易留住。

卡片1｜快时尚产业与生产
从 sweatshop 血汗工厂到 supply chain 供应链，把"快时尚怎么造出来的"这条线的词一次串起来。

卡片2｜消费主义与购物心理
impulse buying、retail therapy、status symbol、peer pressure —— 描述"为什么大家忍不住买买买"的核心词都在这里。

卡片3｜可持续时尚与环保
sustainable、upcycle、carbon footprint、circular economy，写环保类议论文讨论时尚产业时直接能用。

卡片4｜个人风格与身份表达
从 self-expression 自我表达到 authenticity 真实性，覆盖"穿衣打扮体现个性"这个常考角度。

卡片5｜广告营销与消费者行为
influencer、subliminal、planned obsolescence、manipulate consumer behavior，讨论广告如何影响消费的核心词一网打尽。

高频写作搭配：

① the rise of fast fashion
"The rise of fast fashion has led to unprecedented levels of overproduction and textile waste."

② impulse buying driven by peer pressure
"Many young consumers engage in impulse buying driven by peer pressure and the desire for status symbols."

③ planned obsolescence
"Critics argue that planned obsolescence forces consumers into a cycle of constant, unnecessary spending."

④ a more sustainable, circular economy
"Thrift stores and upcycling initiatives are helping shift the fashion industry toward a more sustainable, circular economy."

收藏这组，下次写到 fashion / shopping / consumer behavior 相关话题，就不用只会写 buy clothes 和 like shopping 了。
