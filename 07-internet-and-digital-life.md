## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 动词短语 > 单个词汇**
雅思高分作文不是堆砌单词，而是用动词短语驱动句子。互联网话题几个必备动词短语：
- **go viral** (爆红) — The clip went viral within hours of being posted.
- **breach privacy** (侵犯隐私) — These apps have been accused of breaching user privacy.
- **disrupt traditional industries** (颠覆传统行业) — Streaming platforms have fundamentally disrupted traditional broadcasting.

**2. 网络安全话题写作三件套**
雅思 Task 2 讨论互联网负面影响时，以下三个词串联逻辑最清晰：
**vulnerability（漏洞）→ breach（泄露）→ threat（威胁）**
例：*The inherent vulnerability of online systems creates a persistent threat of data breaches, which can expose millions of users to identity theft and cybercrime.*

**3. on-demand 和 subscription 的区别用法**
口语 Part 3 谈娱乐习惯时常考：
- *on-demand* 强调"随时随地"的便利性 → *On-demand streaming has replaced the rigid TV schedule.*
- *subscription* 强调"付费模式" → *Subscription-based services now dominate the digital entertainment landscape.*
