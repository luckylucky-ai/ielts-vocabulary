## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：讨论能源话题时，"the transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy sources" 是万能开头句型；描述资源问题用 "the depletion of non-renewable resources" 和 "equitable allocation of scarce resources" 比单独的 "run out of resources" 更精准、更学术。
2. **口语加分技巧**：Part 3 谈政府政策时，用 "phase out"、"decarbonize"、"incentivize households to conserve energy" 代替 "stop using" 和 "save energy"，能明显体现词汇的学术层次（Lexical Resource）。
3. **同义替换练习**：把 "we should save energy and use clean energy" 改写为 "households can curb wasteful consumption by retrofitting insulation, while governments accelerate the transition to renewable, sustainable sources" —— 一句话串起 curb / wasteful / retrofit / accelerate / sustainable 五个高阶词。
