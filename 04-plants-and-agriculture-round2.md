## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **写作高分搭配**：讨论农业可持续议题时，"climate-smart agriculture combines crop diversification with early warning systems to reduce yield volatility" 比 "farmers use new technology to deal with bad weather" 更学术；讨论粮食安全政策时，"export tariffs and import quotas are often used to stabilize domestic commodity prices during periods of scarcity" 是 Task 2 经济类话题的高分句型。
2. **口语加分技巧**：谈论农业话题时，别再只说 "farmers grow crops"，试试 "smallholders in this region rely on cooperatives to gain better access to distribution networks" 或 "gene banks preserve the genetic diversity that makes future crop breeding possible"，这类词能明显提升 Lexical Resource 得分。
3. **区分近义词**：`heirloom`（传家宝品种，指世代传承、未经现代杂交改良的老品种）vs `landrace`（地方品种，指长期适应某一地区自然环境形成的本土群体，未必被刻意保存）；`subsidy`（政府补贴，鼓励生产）vs `tariff`（关税，限制进口），两者常在雅思阅读的农业贸易政策段落中被放在一起对比考查。
