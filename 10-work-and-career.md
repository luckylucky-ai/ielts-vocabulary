## 💡 学习小贴士

**1. 高频动词搭配（写作必备）**
- *apply for* a position / *land* a promotion / *meet* a deadline / *take the initiative*
- 写作示例：*Employees who proactively upskill and demonstrate strong competency are far more likely to land a promotion within a competitive hierarchy.*

**2. 雅思写作 Task 2 万用句型**
- The increasing prevalence of **remote** and **hybrid** working arrangements has fundamentally altered the **work-life balance** debate, with many employees prioritising **autonomy** and **flexibility** over salary **compensation**.
- 话题适用：科技影响、社会变化、个人与工作

**3. 口语 Part 1/2 话题词积累**
- "Describe a job you would like to have" → 用 *aspiration, expertise, advancement, compensation, trajectory* 展开
- "Do you prefer working independently or in a team?" → 用 *collaboration, delegate, initiative, accountable* 分析利弊
- 注意：*entrepreneurship* 比 *starting a business* 更学术；*burnout* 比 *being tired from work* 更地道

背单词最怕的是什么？背了就忘，忘了再背，陷入死循环。

今天的话题是「工作与职业」，覆盖求职到创业的完整职场路径，5张卡片，80个高频词。

**5张卡片场景一览：**
- 卡片1｜求职与申请：resume / vacancy / headhunter / onboarding…
- 卡片2｜职场日常：hierarchy / deadline / appraisal / overtime…
- 卡片3｜职业发展：promotion / trajectory / milestone / redundancy…
- 卡片4｜工作与生活平衡：burnout / hybrid / autonomy / decompression…
- 卡片5｜商业与创业：entrepreneur / disruptive / merger / acquisition…

**写作/口语高频搭配精选：**

① **land a promotion** 获得晋升
→ *Employees who consistently demonstrate initiative are more likely to land a promotion.*

② **meet a deadline** 赶上截止日期
→ *Managing a heavy workload while meeting every deadline requires strong organisational skills.*

③ **take the initiative** 主动出击
→ *Rather than waiting to be delegated tasks, she took the initiative to propose a new project.*

④ **strike a work-life balance** 实现工作与生活的平衡
→ *Flexible and remote working arrangements help employees strike a healthier work-life balance.*
