## 💡 学习小贴士

1. **Writing Task 2 必考话题：** AI 几乎是当前雅思写作最高频的新兴话题。常见题型包括 "To what extent should AI replace human workers?"、"Do the benefits of AI outweigh the risks?" 掌握本期词汇后，你可以写出 "While automation may render certain occupations obsolete, it simultaneously generates entrepreneurial opportunities and necessitates workforce upskilling" 这样的高分句。

2. **正反论证万能搭配：** 正方——`boost productivity`（提升生产力）、`streamline logistics`（优化物流）、`achieve unprecedented precision in medical diagnosis`（在医疗诊断中达到前所未有的精准度）。反方——`exacerbate job displacement`（加剧就业替代）、`perpetuate algorithmic bias`（延续算法偏见）、`undermine individual privacy through pervasive surveillance`（通过无处不在的监控侵蚀个人隐私）。

3. **口语 Part 3 高分表达：** 考官问 "How will AI change our lives?" 你可以说："AI is bringing about a paradigm shift in virtually every sector. The key dilemma, I think, is striking a balance between technological innovation and ethical safeguards — we need robust governance frameworks to ensure accountability." 一段话包含 5 个 7.5+ 级别词汇。
